# Squelch — product identity and interface design direction

**Status:** product name locked; visual treatment proposed, not yet an approved final mark or palette.

## Product identity

Squelch is a professional touch-first digital DJ workstation: minimum four simultaneous decks, no mandatory turntables/controllers, rigorously measured response and eventual production/DAW capabilities.

- Name: **Squelch**; lowercase `squelch-*` for internal Rust packages.
- Brand territory: tactile signal manipulation, sound-pressure dynamics, precision and direct control.
- Proposed graphic mark: original phase-shift or compressed audio signal geometry, clear at 16px and legible without gradients.
- Proposed UI: charcoal/obsidian, high-contrast type and meters, vivid signal accents tied to deck identity; A/B/C/D letters always accompany colors.
- Responsive touch targets, meaningful low-latency feedback, reduced motion, complete keyboard accessibility, and no nonessential UI work on the audio thread.
- The final mark, color palette, product-level performance claims and trademark availability are not yet verified.

## Scope exclusions

No pet, mascot, character, companion, confectionery imagery, or related subsystem. Previous Phatcake brand and character concepts are superseded; they are not product assets. Squelch is professional audio software, not a pet application.
