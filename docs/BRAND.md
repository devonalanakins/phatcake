# Phatcake — identity and visual direction

The named concept is Phatcake, with lowercase **phatcake** in public-facing wordmarks and the phrase **Touch the sound.** The concept artwork is exploratory; no vector master, trademark clearance or registered mark is asserted.

- **Core mark:** a custom, visually legible circular loop combining a vinyl platter, audio waveform and frosting-inspired undulation; not merely a stock donut.
- **Palette:** charcoal `#0D0B10`, cream `#FFF1DF`, peach `#FFC2A0`, pink `#FF6EC7`, orange `#FF7A00`, magenta `#D300A8`, occasional zest `#C6FF3D`.
- **Deck identification:** A pink `#FF4DCB`, B orange `#FF9A00`, C green `#7DFF4E`, D cyan `#34E0FF`. Always display deck letters as well as colors.
- **Personality:** playful candy-coated brand, professionally precise audio controls.
- **Performance UI:** low-glare dark surfaces, readable technical typography, generous touch targets, restrained GPU animation. Graphics must degrade gracefully rather than interrupt sound.

The earlier concept imagery is reference material, not a production asset or basis for unverified technical performance claims.
